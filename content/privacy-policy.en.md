This site respects and protects the privacy of its visitors. Below is an explanation of how it handles data.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub Inc.). When you visit, GitHub records certain technical information as part of its standard practice (such as IP address, browser type, and access time) to keep the service secure and running properly — see GitHub's [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for details.

## Advertising & Cookies

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its third-party advertising partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your prior visits to this or other sites. You can manage your personalized ad preferences at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), or opt out of personalized ads from certain third-party vendors at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## On-Site Tools

The tools under the "Tools" section of this site — the Europe duty-free calculator, the secondhand watch valuator, and others — run entirely client-side. Everything you enter is calculated locally in your browser and is never sent, uploaded, or stored on any server.

## Analytics

This site may use web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to measure aggregate information like visit counts, traffic sources, and common device types, in order to improve the content. This data is not used to identify any specific individual.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out via the [contact page](https://junpingkoch-web.github.io/watch-guide-blog/contact/) (it also has an English section further down).

This policy may be updated as the site's content or applicable regulations change; the version published on this page is always the current one.
